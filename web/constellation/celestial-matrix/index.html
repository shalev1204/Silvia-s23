Cartographer's Field Notes

Direct explanations of the sixteen celestial guides found in The Planisphere.

The 4 Astrolabe Pointer Stars
POLARIS (North)
The North Star. It never moves from its place in the sky, sitting directly above the North Pole. Used to anchor true North.
ALDEBARAN (East)
The fiery red eye of Taurus the Bull. It rises prominently in the east during autumn and winter nights.
ALTAIR (South)
The brilliant bright star of the Flying Eagle constellation (Aquila), sweeping across the southern evening sky.
VEGA (West)
The sparkling blue-white jewel of Lyra the Harp. It shines directly overhead before tilting toward the western horizon.
Other Notable Stars
BETELGEUSE
The giant red star marking the glowing right shoulder of Orion the Hunter.
SIRIUS
The Dog Star: the brightest individual star in the entire night sky, following Orion.
Constellations & Star Clusters
CASSIOPEIA
The queen's throne constellation, famous for its distinct giant 'W' shape in the northern sky.
ANDROMEDA
The vast constellation next to Cassiopeia, home to the closest giant spiral galaxy to our own.
ORION
The great Hunter constellation, instantly recognized by the three bright stars forming his belt.
PLEIADES
The Seven Sisters: a tight, glittering cluster of young blue stars resting on the shoulder of Taurus.
Celestial Mechanics & Instruments
SYZYGY
A straight-line alignment of three celestial bodies in space (such as the Sun, Moon, and Earth during an eclipse).
ZENITH
The point in the sky located directly above your head (pointing straight up at a 90-degree angle).
NADIR
The exact opposite of the Zenith: the invisible point directly beneath your feet, down through the earth.
ALIDADE
The rotating brass sight arm mounted on the back of an astrolabe, used to aim at a star and measure its altitude.
SOLSTICE
The day of the year when the Sun reaches its highest or lowest point in the sky at noon (longest/shortest day).
ECLIPSE
The moment when a celestial body blocks the light of another (such as the Moon covering the Sun). The emblem of our Order.
