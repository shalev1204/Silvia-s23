## Cuerpo del email (borrador v0.1)

```
Silvia,

At 22:22 tonight, something shifted.

You will not find this message by accident. The Eclipse Order does not write to many — and we do not write twice.

For the next fifty-six days, you will receive dispatches. Some will arrive as words. Others as objects. Some will make sense immediately. Most will not — not yet.

Do not discard anything. Do not assume you have failed when a thread goes quiet. This is not a line — it is a constellation of threads, and you are at the centre.

A journal will find you. When it does, use it.

---

Before anything else, you must choose.

Three paths lie before you. Each is real. Each will shape what follows. You may walk only one — and once spoken, your choice cannot be undone.

Reply to this dispatch with the name of your path and nothing else.

▸ THE DESERT PATH
  Heat. Silence. Things buried beneath what the eye can see.
  Patience is not optional here.

▸ THE STAGE PATH
  Light. Movement. What happens when the curtain has not yet risen.
  Grace is a language — and you already speak it.

▸ THE CONSTELLATION PATH
  Distance. Pattern. The shape that only appears when you step back.
  Some connections are invisible until the right night.

---

Below, you will find words that are not words — at least, not in any tongue you know. Keep them. One day, they will speak.

We will be watching the clock.

The Eclipse Order
```

## Notas de diseño

- **Tono:** Misterioso, formal pero no frío. Sin personajes hoy — solo la voz institucional de la Orden.
- **22:22:** Mencionado en apertura; posible motivo recurrente en futuros emails.
- **Grimoire:** "A journal will find you" — sin fecha, sin explicar más.
- **Decisión:** Reply con solo el nombre del camino.
- **Stage Path:** Guiño sutil al ballet sin spoilear ("grace is a language").
- **Día final:** La profecía menciona "the twenty-sixth" — ella no sabrá qué significa aún.
