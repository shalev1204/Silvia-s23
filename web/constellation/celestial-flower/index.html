The Sacred Anatomy of the Flower

✦
"The vault does not yield to blind reaching. Twenty-two stars form the petals and stem of the Celestial Flower. Their light answers only when awakened through the four movements of its bloom."
Movement I · The Crown Petals & Core Crown Anchors
The bloom awakes at the ancient Apex (Deneb). The crown unfolds across the western petal (Azelfafage) and eastern petal (Alrisha), anchored through the Corolla Base (Omicron). From these sacred upper petals, the central vein plunges straight into the Blossom Heart (Sadr).
Movement II · The Western Petals Outer Arch & Petal Curves
From the Blossom Heart, the petals extend west along the shoulder (Delta Cygni) and curve (Fawaris) to the outer petal tip (Iota Cygni). The path sweeps inward through the three petal edges (Kappa, Lambda, Mu), curving past the inner flank (Rho Cygni) back into the Heart.
Movement III · The Eastern Petals Mirrored Petals
Mirroring the west, the bloom surges east through the shoulder (Theta Cygni) and curve (Gienah) to the outer petal tip (Zeta Cygni). The starlight traces the three petal edges (Aljanah, Nu, Xi) along the flank (Sigma Cygni) to fold the petals home into the Heart.
Movement IV · The Slender Stem & Golden Root Beacon of Distant Light
With all petals balanced, the flower plunges downward from the Heart along its slender stem (Eta Cygni), arching through the stem node (Flower Crown) to ignite the golden root beacon at its base (Albireo).
