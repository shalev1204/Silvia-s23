Aword, before the silence returns. The Order was not built for kings. It was built for those who notice: the grain of sand, the breath on glass, the star that arrives late.

We do not call you by accident. Someone petitioned the Threshold on your behalf, someone who believed you would not turn away from wonder.

Three guardians await. They disagree about everything. They agree on you. What follows is the first step toward what was hidden.

Three roads lie before you. You will walk all of them.

Some things will arrive. Some will wait. Keep everything.

A journal will find you when you are ready.

THE CONSTELLATION PATH

What only forms when you stop looking directly.

Reply to this dispatch with the name of ONE path (nothing else).
